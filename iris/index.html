Meet Iris

Search your photos by meaning, clear out duplicates and junk, and pick your best shots. All on this device, all private.

1
Download the AI once

Google's EmbeddingGemma 2 vision model, about 388 MB. Use Wi‑Fi. After that it works offline.

2
Pick your photo folders

Choose one parent folder like DCIM or Pictures, so Chrome asks only once. Iris only reads what you pick.

3
Let it look at everything

The first pass takes a while on big libraries. Keep the tablet charging with Iris open. New photos are quick after that.

Search works in English, Spanish and 100+ languages. Google's MediaPipe library may send anonymous usage metrics; your photos never leave the device.
